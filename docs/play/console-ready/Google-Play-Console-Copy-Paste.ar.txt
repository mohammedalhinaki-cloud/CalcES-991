5) Full description — English (copy the following section)
CalcES 991 by Maaoun Studio is a scientific calculator for study and everyday mathematical work. Write fractions, powers, and roots naturally, then tap inside the expression to edit it.

Choose Arabic or English on first launch. Your choice is saved on your device, with menus, tools, and calculation messages in your selected language.

Features:
• Basic arithmetic, percentages, powers, roots, and factorials.
• Trigonometric and inverse functions, logarithms, and DEG/RAD/GRA angle units.
• Numerical definite integration, symbolic differentiation, and evaluation at a point.
• A bracketed numerical root solver, quadratic and cubic polynomials, and linear systems with up to three unknowns.
• Matrix determinants, inverses, and multiplication within supported sizes.
• Complex numbers in Cartesian and polar form.
• Mean, standard deviation, and linear regression.
• BIN/OCT/DEC/HEX conversion and signed 32-bit bitwise operations.
• The last 50 successful calculations in the current session, variable memory, and Ans.
• A touch-editable display and a responsive keypad that fits the phone screen.

Calculator functions work offline without an account or sign-in. Language and rating reminder preferences stay on your device. Expressions are not sent to a calculation server. Version 1.1.0, build 4, displays no ads and includes no in-app purchases.

Numerical computations have finite precision. Integration and root finding require appropriate domains and convergence conditions. Use the app as an educational aid and independently verify important results. This is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CASIO.
