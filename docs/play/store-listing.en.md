Title: CalcES 991 - حاسبة علمية

Short description:
Offline scientific calculator with touch math editing, calculus and matrices.

Full description:
Write natural mathematical expressions and edit any part by touch.

CalcES 991 - حاسبة علمية combines a scientific calculator with an interactive math editor. Calculations run on your device, and calculator functions work offline after installation.

• Arithmetic, fractions, powers, roots, percentages and factorials.
• Trigonometry and inverse functions in degrees, radians and gradians.
• Numerical definite integration and symbolic differentiation with point evaluation.
• A bracketed numerical root, linear systems of up to three unknowns, and polynomials up to degree three.
• Matrix determinants, inverses and multiplication, with 2×2 and 3×3 input grids.
• Complex numbers, polar input, statistics and linear regression.
• Binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal with signed 32-bit logical operations.
• Variables, Ans and session history with calculation recall.
• A responsive layout with collapsible advanced tools.

Numerical precision is finite. Integration and root solving have domain and convergence requirements. No account is required.

When advertising is enabled, a single adaptive banner appears at the top. There are no full-screen or rewarded ads. Connectivity is needed for advertising and consent updates, not for the calculations themselves.
